Privacy Policy — Bridal Glow: Wedding Dress Up

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Bridal Glow ("the app", "we") is a single-player dress-up and makeover game. This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

Information we collect

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, photos or location on our servers. We do not have any servers.
  • Game progress (coins, unlocked items, saved looks, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Photos you save from the game are images of your in-game styled character. They stay on your device (app storage or your gallery, if you choose to save there). They are shared only when you use the share button.

Advertising

  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob.
  • To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
  • device and advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / App Set ID)
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location)
  • app interaction and performance/diagnostic data
  • This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob policies.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings: Settings › Google › Ads.

Children

  • Bridal Glow is designed for a general audience of ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13.
  • If we learn that a user is under 13, the app requests only non-personalized, child-appropriate ads (G rated).

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Aside from the ad processing described above, which Google performs, we do not share data with third parties.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device. To delete it:

  • use Settings › Reset progress in the app, or
  • uninstall the app.

For ad data, use your Android ad settings (see above).

Security

Data in transit to Google's ad services is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
